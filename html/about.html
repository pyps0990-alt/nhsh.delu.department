關於我們

此網站的設計目的，是為了讓各位能夠在午餐前預定午餐，不至於發生熱食部排不到隊的情況，讓每位內中人都能夠享受午餐的美好時光。

This website is designed to let everyone pre-order lunch before lunchtime, preventing situations where people can't get into the cafeteria line, and ensuring that everyone at our school can enjoy a pleasant lunch.
